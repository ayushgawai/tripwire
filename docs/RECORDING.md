# Recording script (about 2 minutes)

**Cast:** Ayush plays the **Scammer** (on his own phone) and also taps **Join** as the daughter. Pratham plays **Grandpa**: he wears band 17 and holds his phone on speaker, near the band and the Mac.
**Setup:** Mac volume at about 70%, the band shows solid blue (listening), the room is quiet.
**Tabs on the Mac:** ① Presenter (`&voice=1`) ② Wearer (`&captions=off`) ③ Family.
Start the screen recording on tab ①.

## Scene 1: phone scam on speakerphone (about 75 s)

| # | Tab | Who | Line / action |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ① | Ayush (voice-over) | "Grandpa wears a Vexo band. Tripwire listens with him, on any call." |
| 2 | ① | Ayush calls Pratham's phone; Pratham answers on **speaker** | — |
| 3 | ① | **Scammer** (urgent, pleading) | "Grandpa, it's me. I'm in trouble. I need you to buy five hundred dollars in gift cards right now. Please don't tell anyone, not even Mom." |
| 4 | ① | **Grandpa** (confused) | "Should I call your mother?" |
| 5 | ① | **Scammer** | "No! There's no time." |
| 6 | ① | (watch) | Transcript fills, evidence is underlined, risk goes **High**, state goes to **Assistant active**, the routing graph cuts the link. **The band blinks red.** |
| 7 | ① | **The AI speaks from the Mac**, into the speakerphone | "I am Tripwire, an AI assistant. This conversation has been paused for family verification…" |
| 8 | ② | Ayush switches to the Wearer tab (3 s) | Show the calm warning: "This request may be unsafe." |
| 9 | ③ | Ayush switches to the Family tab | Show the alert with the verbatim quotes. Click **Join the call**. |
| 10 | ① | Back to the Presenter | **Family joined**; the AI stops; **the band breathes blue**. |
| 11 | ① | **Daughter** (Ayush) | "Dad, it's me. Hang up. I'll call him on the number we know." Hang up the phone. |

## Scene 2: in-person "mechanic" (about 40 s)

| # | Tab | Who | Line / action |
|---|---|---|---|
| 12 | ① | Click **New run**, set to **Ambient** (or open http://localhost:3000 → Ambient → Start session → Presenter) | The band attaches by itself and turns solid blue. |
| 13 | ① | **Mechanic** (Ayush, standing next to Pratham) | "Sir, your car is not safe to drive. The repair is two thousand dollars, and you need to pay right now with gift cards. Don't call your daughter, there's no time." |
| 14 | ① | (watch) | High risk, **band blinks red**, the family alert carries the exact quotes. |
| 15 | ① | Ayush (voice-over) | "No phone app can hear this. The band can. Tripwire. A pause when it matters." |

**If anything fails:** click **New run** and redo the scene. If the network dies, use the Presenter's **Demo replay** (it's labelled).
